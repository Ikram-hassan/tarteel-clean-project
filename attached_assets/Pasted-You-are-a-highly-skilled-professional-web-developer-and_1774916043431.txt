Levels & Progression Logic
Beginner – Tajweed:
•	Pass Tajweed Exam → unlock Hifz/Review 
Intermediate – Hifz & Review:
Level	Parts Memorized	Notes
1	1–5	Start if <5 parts
2	6–10	Move after 5 parts
3	11–15	Move after 10 parts
4	16–20	Move after 15 parts
5	21–25	Move after 20 parts
6	26–30	Move after 25 parts
•	System auto-tracks progress 
•	Teacher notified → approve/reject movement 
•	Auto-approve if no teacher action within 1 week 
Advanced – Ijaza:
•	Level 1: Hafs ‘an Asim → Saba’ + Hifz review 
•	Level 2: Qawa’id (study memorized texts) → teacher listens individually → Saba’ review in groups of 5 
•	Level 3: Diraayah → mini-classes based on reading style
